The Loop

What you're looking at is a map of how I actually work with AI every day. It is not one chatbot answering questions. It is a whole team, with me in the middle of it — and this picture shows how the pieces connect, and how the whole thing keeps going around.

Me, talking Bubby — chief of staff The specialists The memory Back into my life
The light moving through the lines is work traveling through the system. Click or tap any dot to see what it does.
Step 01 — It starts with me

Me, talking

Everything begins with me. I send a voice message, share a photo, forward a receipt, or just say what I need. It could be “remind me before my class,” or “something's wrong with my computer,” or “help me figure out my budget.” Normal stuff — the way you'd text a friend.

Step 02 — The middle

My AI chief of staff

All of it goes to Bubby, my main AI assistant. Think of him like a really good office manager. He figures out what I'm actually asking for, checks what he already knows about me, and decides what needs to happen. Sometimes he handles it himself. Sometimes he sends it to one of the specialists.

Step 03 — The specialists

Four agents, four jobs

  • Damon watches for extra teaching classes I can pick up, and grabs them before anyone else does. He's a big reason I earn what I earn.
  • Gwen looks after my computer. She keeps an eye on it, fixes problems before I even notice them, and builds the tools I need.
  • Kilo is the night watchman. He keeps watch over everything while I sleep.
  • Bubby also keeps all my records — every dollar tracked, every receipt saved, every appointment remembered.
Step 04 — The memory

It remembers everything

Here's the part most people miss. Every conversation gets saved and sorted. So next week, Bubby still remembers what we talked about today. It's like working with someone who never forgets a thing. The longer we work together, the better it gets — because everything builds on what came before it.

Step 05 — The loop closes

Back to my real life

The agents do their work, and the results come back to me. A reminder pops up before class. My computer runs smooth. Money gets written down. Groceries get tracked. Then I answer, give a new instruction, or ask the next thing — and the whole cycle starts again.

Why it matters

Not a search box. An operation.

Most people use AI like a search box: ask a question, get an answer, done. This is different. This is AI woven into a real person's daily life. I'm not chatting with a robot. I'm running an operation where I'm the boss and the AI handles the rest. Follow the picture above: me → AI → specialists → memory → results → back to me. Around and around, a little smarter every time.
